# Homepage Project Selection Design

## Goal

Keep the homepage focused on representative work instead of duplicating the complete project catalog.

## Approved direction

- Retain the rights-management platform as the featured case.
- Retain two secondary projects in the homepage index: the AI collaboration platform and the travel-benefits ecosystem.
- Remove the multi-tenant mall and physical-mall rows from the homepage only; preserve them in `projects.html` and keep all public case URLs unchanged.
- Replace the quiet text link with a full-width editorial call to action that clearly leads to the complete five-project archive.
- Keep the existing focus/hover preview interaction for the two retained projects and remove only their now-unreachable homepage preview nodes.

## Verification

- Check the homepage at desktop and mobile widths.
- Confirm both retained project rows still switch the visual preview by pointer and keyboard focus.
- Confirm the new call to action reaches `projects.html`.
- Confirm no console errors and no horizontal overflow.
